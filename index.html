How it works

Verified data

Swaps are read from public JSON-RPC (or your own endpoints), proven complete per block range, cross-checked and published to ClickHouse only after quality gates pass. Prices come from stablecoin legs or verified hourly prices; unvalued swaps are never counted as $0.

Analytics and findings

Covered-hour metrics, window comparisons, an anomaly engine with frozen baselines, and reports that state exactly which pools and hours they cover.

Evidence-grounded AI

The AI phrases answers from the verified facts of each question. Every sentence must cite its facts; numbers, terminology and claims are re-checked, and anything unproven is replaced by a deterministic, evidence-backed answer — clearly labelled.

What it does

Networks and protocols

Base, Arbitrum One, OP Mainnet and Ethereum. Uniswap v3-style, Uniswap v2-style and Solidly-style (Velodrome/Aerodrome v2) pools — each family supported only after its decoded swaps matched transaction receipts exactly on several networks.

Universal token explorer

Any token contract: on-chain identity, pool discovery, a support level per pool, collection of only the missing windows, per-pool coverage, quality checks and an evidence-backed report.

Anomaly intelligence

Level shifts against a frozen baseline, plus concentration, imbalance, divergence and repetitive patterns. Every finding shows its facts, method, limitations and alternative explanations.

Reports and comparisons

Markdown, HTML and JSON reports with price-source coverage, pool coverage, protocol breakdown and SQL evidence; window comparisons marked comparable only when every pool is covered in both.

Multilingual AI analyst

Multi-turn conversations with follow-up context, tested in English, Turkish and German. Answers are labelled as a validated AI answer or a deterministic fallback.

AI provider options

Local models via Ollama (tested). Anthropic Claude API and OpenAI API (integrated; optional, off by default, no paid call until an administrator authorises it within limits). Self-hosted OpenAI-compatible servers such as vLLM, llama.cpp or LM Studio (integration option through the same adapter).

Organisations and operations

Organisations, roles, workspaces, saved reports and an audit log; one operations page for service health, pipeline runs, data freshness, quality audits, backups and alerts.

Verified research examples

Aggregator volume anomaly on Base

1 Sep → 15 Oct 2026, audited, 3,293,676 decoded events. Window volume $458.56M, with 90.2% from one automated account; peak 10.3× the pre-onset median; volume excluding that account −15.5% against baseline (corrected by the audit from −20.5%).

ARB on Arbitrum One

3 ingested pools, 14 Oct 12:00 → 17 Oct 03:00 UTC. 63,089 pool swap events, valued volume $17.06M: 36.8% through stablecoin legs, 63.2% through verified hourly prices, 0 unvalued. Pass-through routing 0.1%.

VELO on OP Mainnet

12 pools discovered, all receipt-verified; 6 ingested. The Solidly pools carried 62,302 swap events but only 463,647 VELO, while the v3 pools carried 3,266 events and 12.2 M VELO. Coverage of the ingested pools is complete; market coverage is not, and the report says so.

Findings are statistical observations. They never establish intent, manipulation or wash trading, and addresses are not identified entities.

Limitations

  • Only the listed networks and verified decoder families are analysed, and only pools that were discovered and ingested. Other venues are not counted.
  • The examples cover historical, ingested windows; nothing on this page is live.
  • Local AI quality depends on the model. Validators reject ungrounded or forbidden statements but do not correct grammar.
  • Optional Dune and cloud AI integrations are off by default.
  • DeFi Intelligence is analytics and research software. It is not investment advice, makes no promise of trading or investment outcomes, and is not a regulatory certification.
