How it works

Three stages, each with its own checks. Nothing reaches a report or an AI answer without passing the stage before it.

  1. Verified data

    Ingestion runs prove block coverage, confirm empty answers with a second provider and publish to ClickHouse only after quality gates pass. USD comes from stablecoin legs or an hourly price feed configured for the installation; unvalued swaps are never counted as $0.

  2. Analytics and findings

    Covered-hour metrics, window comparisons and an anomaly engine with frozen baselines. Reports state exactly which pools and hours they cover.

  3. Evidence-grounded AI

    The AI phrases answers from the verified facts of each question. Every number must match a fact the sentence cites; anything unproven is replaced by a clearly labelled deterministic answer.

What teams can do with it today

Three analytical tasks the current release supports on its verified networks and protocol families. Each one states what it covers and what it does not.

Analyse a token pool by pool

Token teams, protocol analytics, research desks

Paste a token contract. The application checks it on-chain, finds its pools and says which ones it can decode. It then collects the missing hours through the same gated pipeline and reports swaps, token volume, buyers and sellers, and routing per pool — with the covered hours stated.

Covers the selected pools on supported networks, not the token's whole market.

Triage unusual activity

Risk, market-integrity and research teams

The anomaly engine flags volume, activity and liquidity shifts against a baseline frozen at onset, plus concentration, imbalance and repetitive patterns. Every finding carries its facts, method, parameters, limitations and alternative explanations, so it can be checked before anyone acts on it.

A statistical observation, never a claim of intent or manipulation.

Compare two time windows

Growth, treasury and research teams

Compare a token's activity before and after a listing, an incentive change or an event. A comparison is marked comparable only when every pool is covered in both windows; otherwise the report says it is incomplete instead of showing a misleading change.

Historical windows that have been ingested; not live streaming.

What a finding is — and is not

  • A statistical observation. A metric moved far from a baseline frozen at onset. The finding states the method, parameters and the data it used.
  • Never an accusation. It does not establish intent, manipulation or wash trading, and addresses are not identified entities.
  • Alternatives stated up front. Market-wide moves, new liquidity venues, a single large participant or a listing can each explain a spike.
  • Coverage is explicit. Only hours covered by successful ingestion runs are analysed; partial market coverage is reported, not hidden.

Limitations

Stated plainly, so you know what the numbers cover.

  • Only the listed networks, verified protocol families and individually verified pools are analysed, and only pools that were discovered and ingested. Other venues are not counted, so no figure on this page is a token's whole market.
  • Pool-event volume counts a routed trade in every ingested pool it passes through. Transaction-net volume is reported separately.
  • The examples on this page cover historical, ingested windows. Nothing here is live, and the page connects to no API.
  • AI answer quality depends on the model. Validators reject ungrounded or forbidden statements but do not correct grammar.
  • Dune and cloud AI integrations are optional and off by default.
  • Off-site backups need an external S3-compatible destination configured per installation; until one is registered and verified, they are reported as not configured. Recovery was verified on our reference installation, not on every customer environment.
  • WATAM DeFi Intelligence is installed per customer on their own infrastructure. A hosted, multi-tenant SaaS service is not offered.
  • The research examples use public blockchain data collected by the application; USD values are omitted because they depend on an external price feed. Protocol, token and network names describe the data analysed; they do not imply any affiliation or endorsement.
  • WATAM DeFi Intelligence is analytics and research software. It is not investment advice, makes no promise of trading or investment outcomes, and is not a regulatory certification.
