How it works

Three stages, each with its own checks. Nothing reaches a report or an AI answer without passing the stage before it.

  1. Verified data

    Ingestion runs prove block coverage, confirm empty answers with a second provider and publish to ClickHouse only after quality gates pass. USD comes from stablecoin legs or verified hourly prices; unvalued swaps are never counted as $0.

  2. Analytics and findings

    Covered-hour metrics, window comparisons and an anomaly engine with frozen baselines. Reports state exactly which pools and hours they cover.

  3. Evidence-grounded AI

    The AI phrases answers from the verified facts of each question. Every number must match a fact the sentence cites; anything unproven is replaced by a clearly labelled deterministic answer.

One workflow from token to report

Start with any token contract on a supported network and end with findings and a report you can defend.

Universal token explorer

Paste a token contract. DeFi Intelligence checks its identity on-chain and against an independent registry, discovers its pools and tells you which ones it can analyse.

  • A support level and reason for every pool
  • Collects only the hours that are missing, through the same gated pipeline
  • Per-pool coverage, quality checks and an evidence-backed report

Anomaly intelligence

Volume, activity and liquidity shifts against a baseline frozen at onset, plus concentration, imbalance, divergence and repetitive patterns.

  • Facts, method and parameters for every finding
  • Limitations and alternative explanations, stated up front
  • Statistical observations — never claims of intent or manipulation

Research and reporting

Reports in Markdown, HTML and JSON with price-source coverage, pool coverage, protocol breakdown, multi-hop reconciliation and the SQL evidence.

  • Window comparisons marked comparable only when every pool is covered in both
  • Workspaces with saved, frozen reports per organisation
  • Pool-event volume and transaction-net volume reported separately

Limitations

Stated plainly, so you know what the numbers cover.

  • Only the listed networks and verified decoder families are analysed, and only pools that were discovered and ingested. Other venues are not counted.
  • The examples on this page cover historical, ingested windows. Nothing here is live, and the page connects to no API.
  • AI answer quality depends on the model. Validators reject ungrounded or forbidden statements but do not correct grammar.
  • Dune and cloud AI integrations are optional and off by default.
  • Off-site backups need an external S3-compatible destination configured per installation; until one is registered and verified, they are reported as not configured.
  • DeFi Intelligence is analytics and research software. It is not investment advice, makes no promise of trading or investment outcomes, and is not a regulatory certification.
